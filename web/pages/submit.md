<h1 class="form-title">Add something in the wild</h1>
<p class="form-dek">Link to the thing itself, not the announcement about the thing. New signals start as Announced until someone has checked that anyone can use them.</p>

<p class="form-rules" role="note">You need to <a href="/sign-in/">sign in</a> and pick a handle first. A submission is public as soon as you send it, with your handle beside it. Editors review submissions after the fact: they can hide one, or promote it to a draft signal.</p>

<h2 id="counts-h" class="rail-h">What counts as a signal</h2>
<ul class="rail-points">
<li>A product or feature a stranger can use today, at a price on the page.</li>
<li>Weights you can download, with the licence stated where the weights are.</li>
<li>A model making decisions, with a public record: a policy, a filing, an audit, published outcomes.</li>
<li>A paper or dataset the map should know about.</li>
</ul>
