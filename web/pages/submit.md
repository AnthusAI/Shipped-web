<h1 class="form-title">Submit something that shipped</h1>
<p class="form-dek">When it opens, link to the thing itself, not the announcement about the thing. If it has a waitlist, it will go in as a claim and probably come out stamped Vapor.</p>
<p class="form-rules" role="note">You will need to <a href="/sign-in/">sign in</a> and pick a handle first. A submission will be public as soon as you send it, with your handle beside it. Editors will review submissions after the fact: they can hide one, or promote it to a draft item.</p>
<p class="form-rules">After you send a link it will show as Pending while we read it. The form will check at once whether the link is already in, or another link from the same site came in recently. “Save draft” will keep your unsent form in this browser only; it will never be sent anywhere, and it will clear when you submit.</p>
