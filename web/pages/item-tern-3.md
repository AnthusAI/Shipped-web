<div class="section-heading"><span class="cintillo">01</span><h2 id="summary-h">Summary</h2></div>
<p>Tern Labs released Tern-3, a 14-billion-parameter model tuned for code completion and repository-level edits. The weights are on a public hub under Apache-2.0, and at 4-bit quantisation it runs on a single 24 GB consumer GPU.</p>
