<section aria-labelledby="summary-h"><div class="section-heading"><span class="cintillo">01</span><h2 id="summary-h">Summary</h2><span class="sample-mark" title="Fictional sample data that previews the design">Sample</span></div>
<div class="prose"><p>Tern Labs released Tern-3, a 14-billion-parameter model tuned for code completion and repository-level edits. The weights are on a public hub under Apache-2.0. At 4-bit quantisation it runs on a single 24 GB consumer GPU with room for a 32k context.</p><p>The release includes the training mix down to the list of source repositories, and the evaluation harness used for the model card. Two of the three headline benchmark suites reproduce within a point; the third depends on a dataset that is still behind a request form.</p></div></section>

<section class="editors-note" aria-labelledby="note-h"><div class="editors-note-head"><h2 id="note-h" class="kicker kicker-ink">Editors&rsquo; note</h2><span class="sample-mark" title="Fictional sample data that previews the design">Sample</span><span class="kicker kicker-ink">The desk &middot; 21 Oct</span></div>
<div class="prose"><p>It is what the card says. We pulled the weights, matched the checksums, and ran the published harness on a single 4090: two suites landed within a point of the claimed numbers.</p><p>Novel it is not. This is a careful refinement of a well-known recipe, and the voters have scored it accordingly. But careful refinements with honest licences are what people deploy, and that is the point of this list.</p></div></section>
