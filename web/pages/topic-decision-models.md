<h1 class="concept-title">Decision models</h1>
<p class="concept-definition">A decision model is a model whose job is to make a judgement (a label, a yes or no, a score, a choice of route) rather than write prose. This page tracks the ones in use in the wild: guard and moderation classifiers, judge and reward models, routers, and models sold as decision models.</p>
